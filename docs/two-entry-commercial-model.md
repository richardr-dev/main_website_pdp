# Operational Resilience: Recovery Health Check first

Implemented on `/` (Indonesian) and `/en` (English). Blue-and-white design, bilingual Insights, first-visit consent, footer, and existing routes are retained.

## Website hierarchy

- Primary: PatuhData Recovery Health Check, starting from Rp5,000,000 for a limited agreed scope. Final price depends on complexity, access, environment, and testing scope.
- Secondary specialist pathway: Financial Vendor Readiness starts with a Bank Vendor Readiness Pack for one customer assessment. Includes evidence mapping and questionnaire support. Missing evidence is recorded honestly; new documentation, additional tests, and implementation are separately scoped.
- Assess → Fix → Manage: shared process for both entry points.
- Follow-on work: BCP/DR Test & Evidence Pack, Backup & Recovery Remediation, Security Baseline Hardening, and Managed Resilience.
- Managed Resilience modules: backup health and recovery tests; access/patch reviews; evidence and plan maintenance; scoped incident support. Hours, cadence, responsibilities, and response targets must be agreed.

The primary hero/header CTA books Recovery Health Check. Financial Vendor Readiness has a secondary text link and a separate outline-button section. Each booking selects the correct service in the inquiry form. The starter offer is directly below the hero. The report is explicitly illustrative and includes system, scope, date, environment, method, limitations, result, and validation owner.

## Commercial model

The owner explicitly authorized the Rp5,000,000 starting price in the current brief. This is not a fixed price for every assessment. No starting prices for other services or assessment-credit promise are published. Tax treatment, detailed quantities, and timing belong in the written proposal.

Quotation structure requested by the owner:

- Assessment: paid upfront before work begins, after written scope acceptance.
- Project/remediation: deposit before licence, hardware, or procurement commitments. Specify deposit amount, balance milestones, cancellation terms, and procurement coverage in the quotation.
- Managed service: monthly or quarterly in advance according to agreement, with service modules, hours, response targets, and responsibilities defined.

The website collects inquiries, not payments. It does not place procurement orders or imply PatuhData finances customer infrastructure.

## Inquiry handling

Required fields: name, company, role, work email, WhatsApp/phone, staff count, need, and critical system/customer requirement. Optional fields: deadline and environment. The five need choices are Recovery Health Check, Financial Vendor Readiness, BCP / DR Test, Managed Resilience, and Not sure / discuss first.

Form data goes to the existing Web3Forms integration. The privacy notice warns against submitting credentials, production data, or sensitive personal information. Consent-gated analytics receives conversion events, not form values. Actual delivery still needs a production-provider check; automated tests use mocks.

## Website lifecycle

Business problem → Assess → Findings + evidence → Fix → Validate → Manage → Retest + improve. Findings determine follow-on recovery, security, infrastructure, and documentation work. Existing blue/white design, bilingual content, Insights, technology logos, footer, consent controls, and legal routes remain.

## Regulatory boundaries

Financial customer requirements and direct regulatory obligations are different. The homepage does not imply that every bank vendor is an ITSK provider, promise bank approval, or advertise a blanket December 2026 compliance deadline. Regulatory filings, legal opinions, and certification are excluded from the entry pack.
